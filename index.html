About

I find joy in shaping impactful stories, the kind that make people think and keep watching until the very last frame.

I'm an editor and filmmaker from Venezuela. With a background in Fine Arts and Music, and a Master's in Film Editing, I build stories from the footage up, even when they aren't obvious at first.

I work across scripted and unscripted content, pairing a strong editorial eye with hands-on knowledge of the whole post pipeline: editing, motion graphics, visual effects, sound design, color and finishing. I've also worked as an assistant editor, post coordinator and post supervisor, and I teach editing and documentary editing at the Academy of Art University.

Whether I'm shaping a narrative or crafting eye-catching content, I bring creativity, versatility and a thoughtful approach to every project.
